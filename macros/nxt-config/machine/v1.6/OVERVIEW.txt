Milo v1.6 machine pack (nxt)
============================
Homing requirements: docs/NXT_BOARD_HOMING.md (v1.6 — Y toward Y0, Z reversed, Z800).

This tree mirrors the nxt v1.5 board layout; edit under nxt-config/machine/v1.6/
for Milo v1.6 without disturbing v1.5 or v2.0 users.

Automatic pack load (opt-in) runs from nxt.g after nxt-vars.g and nxt-user-vars.g
via macros/system/nxt-board-pack-loader.g — see docs/NXT_BOARD_CONFIG.md.

1. nxt Configuration: Platform v1.6, bootstrap Auto, Save (writes nxt-user-vars.g and nxt-board-bootstrap.requested).
2. Optionally create 0:/sys/nxt-board-bootstrap.skip to hard-disable pack load.
3. For motor-variant boards: global nxtBoardMotorVoltage = 24 or 48 in nxt-user-vars.g.
4. For a fully manual chain, add 0:/sys/nxt-user-board.g with M98 P"nxt-config/board/.../entry.g"
   (see macros/system/nxt-user-board.g.example).

config.g: For a thin install, call M98 P"nxt.g" early and avoid duplicating M98 lines for
drives/limits/spindle if the board pack loader runs them.
